Radical Honesty FAQ

Can interviewers detect this?▼
No. Canopy runs as a native overlay using OS-level APIs. No browser extension. No suspicious window switching. No mouse jitter. No alt-tab. We tested against 10 popular proctoring tools — zero detections.
What video apps does it work with?▼
All of them. Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Skype, Discord, Slack Huddles. We capture at the system audio level (WASAPI loopback on Windows, BlackHole on Mac), so the app doesn't matter.
Do my answers go to the cloud?▼
Only if you want them to. Use the Local mode and everything stays on your machine — transcription (faster-whisper), reasoning (Ollama), the works. Zero network calls. For cloud mode, you bring your own API keys. We never see them. We never store them. They live in your local .env file.
How many languages?▼
50+ including English, Spanish, Mandarin, Hindi, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, and more. Our STT uses faster-whisper which outperforms cloud APIs on non-English speech.
What if I get caught or it doesn't work?▼
7-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. But here's the thing — we can't prevent you from being caught if you're careless (talking while "typing," obvious eye movement). Our job is making the tool invisible. Your job is acting natural. Use wisely.
